Privacy Policy — Business Machine

Effective: September 2026

Business Machine is built by Small Engine Studios to run entirely on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means for your privacy.

The short version

We collect nothing. The app makes no network requests, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

Business profiles, machine values, notes, presets, and scenarios you create are stored only on your device. JSON and PDF exports are generated locally, by you, for you — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

In-app purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play. Payment information is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see, handle, or store your payment details.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
