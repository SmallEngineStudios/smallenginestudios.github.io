Meet Weldon

Weldon is our shop foreman, our mascot, and our conscience. He’s a welder the size of a toolbox — a small engine — and he runs the studio the way he runs his torch: steady, honest, no wasted motion.

He is the studio’s argument made flesh: you don’t need to be big to be load-bearing. His mask stays flipped up because he’d rather look at the work than hide behind it. His torch is oversized because he’d rather carry the right tool than the convenient one. And the thumbs-up isn’t approval — it’s structural. It means it’ll hold.

Field notes from the shop

  • Keeps every bolt he’s ever removed. “Just in case” has never once been wrong.
  • Talks to machines like they’re listening. They’ve never complained.
  • Pink cheeks: forge heat. Or embarrassment. He won’t say.
  • Ask what he’s building and he’ll show you the weld, never the blueprint.
  • Nobody has seen Weldon sleep. The shop light is always on.
