How the studio works

Small Engine Studios is a small team: one human director, and an AI dev.

That’s not an ideology — it’s circumstance. I’m one ambitious person. I’ve built robotics, dug into code, written storyboards, made music. But I never learned to build apps, and life hasn’t presented room to start. I still wanted to make things that help people — tools that do real work, or games worth your evening.

So the AI writes the code. I make every call: what gets built, how it works, what ships and what doesn’t. Nothing gets built without my approval first. The vision, the taste, the decisions — those are mine.

And I’m not only steering. I’ve spent 100s of hours hand-drawing art and producing music for this studio — time I chose to put where it can’t be replaced. Speed where speed is fine; human hands where it matters.

AI’s a wonderful tool, not dissimilar from picking the square or circle in MS Paint — but the product you get with me is unmistakably different than what would have been made without me.

And the door’s open — if human collaborators want in, or the market asks for all-human projects, I’m here for it. The goal will never change: make things that help.
